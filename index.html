About Me

I am a machine learning engineer specializing in NLP and speech technologies.I have experience with AI consulting companies, where I have worked on designing AI-driven solutions, including knowledge graphs, large language models (LLMs), and chatbots to optimize decision-making and automation in various industries.

With my background in chemical engineering, I am passionate about knowledge-driven AI solutions, and optimizing chemical engineering processes.

Beyond my technical pursuits. I explore innovative solutions to enhance industrial processes, focusing on sustainability, safety, and productivity. My interests extend to bioinformatics and protein engineering, where I study protein structures and leverage AI for biological research.

When I am not coding, I love reading research papers related and unrelated to my field.

I am always looking to explore the intersection of AI with my diverse interests, whether in engineering, biotechnology, or research-driven innovations. This curiosity drives my continuous learning and the projects I take on.

I am also deeply involved in tech communities, serving as the Public Relations Officer (PRO) of the Space Club and as a lead in DSN OAU (Data Science Network, Obafemi Awolowo University).

Through my community involvement, I aim to make it easier for people like me, who experience anxiety, to navigate the tech industry. I understand the challenges of breaking into technical spaces and want to create supportive environments that foster growth, learning, and inclusivity.

Projects

Let Me Cook 🍲

A recipe generation tool that creates meals based on available pantry ingredients. Developed using a self-created dataset featuring recipes from personal sources and a Nigerian food blogger, Sisi Yemmie. Aimed at solving the everyday challenge of deciding what to cook.

Key Features: Ingredient-based recipe generation, personalized meal suggestions

Green Watch 🌿

An innovative project that checks compliance of Nigerian reports and papers with environmental regulations. Involved extensive data collection and self-annotation from NE datasets, highlighting my interest in environmental engineering and industry sustainability.

Challenges: Time-intensive annotation process

Chem Sum 🧪

A summarization tool for chemical engineering papers, bridging my academic background with machine learning expertise. Initially developed with an external dataset, then pivoted to focus on Nigerian contexts.

Focus: Technical paper summarization, domain-specific NLP

Petrophysical Reservoir Characterization 📊

A collaborative research project predicting porosity of reservoir tanks to assess profitability. Involved intensive research, reading over 30 papers in less than a week, and resulting in a research paper submission.

Achievements: Comprehensive literature review, collaborative research paper
