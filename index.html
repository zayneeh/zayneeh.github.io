About Me

I am a machine learning engineer specializing in NLP and speech technologies. I have experience with AI consulting companies, where I have worked on designing AI-driven solutions to optimize decision-making and automation in various industries.

With my background in chemical engineering, I am passionate about knowledge-driven AI solutions and optimizing industrial processes. My interests extend to Polymer informatics and protein engineering, where I study protein structures and leverage AI for Polymer research. I am also drawn to sustainability, focusing on creating efficient systems that not only improve productivity but are also environmentally friendly.

Beyond my technical work, I am a very curious person, so I’m always thinking about a million things at once, and writing helps me make sense of them. Whether it's AI, science, or something completely random, I love exploring connections between my interests. I document my thoughts and discoveries on Medium.

Reading various research papers is another way I explore how my various interests intersect, from engineering and beyond.

I am also actively involved in tech communities, serving as the Public Relations Officer (PRO) of the Space Club and as a Deep learning lead in DSN OAU (Data Science Network, Obafemi Awolowo University). Through my community involvement, I aim to make it easier for people like me—especially those who experience anxiety—to navigate the tech industry.
