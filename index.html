About Me

I am a machine learning engineer specializing in NLP and speech technologies. I have experience working with AI consulting companies, where I design AI-driven solutions that support decision-making and automation across different industries.

With a background in chemical engineering, I also explore how machine learning helps us understand molecular systems in materials. My research explores this through enzymes and proteins, and more recently through polymers, where I use ML to study structure–property relationships and material behavior.

Beyond technical work, I'm a very curious person so I enjoy exploring connections between AI, science, food, and how cooking intersects and I document my thoughts and discoveries on Medium.
